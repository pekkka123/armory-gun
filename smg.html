SMG Collection

Thompson M1921 (Tommy Gun)

The Thompson submachine gun (also known as the "Tommy gun", "Chicago typewriter", or "trench broom") is a blowback-operated, selective-fire submachine gun developed by Brigadier General John T. Thompson in 1918.

Action: Blowback, selective fire

Caliber: .45 ACP

Type: Classic Submachine Gun (SMG)

Magazine Capacity: 20, 30, or 50 rounds

Country of Origin: United States

Year Introduced: 1921

MP 40

The MP 40 is a German submachine gun chambered for the 9×19mm Parabellum cartridge and used extensively during World War II.

Action: Blowback, selective fire

Caliber: 9×19mm Parabellum

Type: Classic Submachine Gun

Magazine Capacity: 32 rounds

Country of Origin: Germany

Year Introduced: 1940

Uzi

The Uzi is a compact Israeli submachine gun developed after the Arab-Israeli War of 1948 and became one of the most recognizable SMGs worldwide.

Action: Blowback, selective fire

Caliber: 9×19mm Parabellum

Type: Compact Submachine Gun

Magazine Capacity: 25, 32, or 40 rounds

Country of Origin: Israel

Year Introduced: 1950

HK MP5

The Heckler & Koch MP5 is one of the most famous modern submachine guns, known for reliability and widespread military and police use.

Action: Roller-delayed blowback

Caliber: 9×19mm Parabellum

Type: Modern SMG

Magazine Capacity: 15, 30, or 40 rounds

Country of Origin: Germany

Year Introduced: 1966

FN P90

The FN P90 is a Belgian personal defense weapon featuring a unique top-mounted 50-round magazine and compact futuristic design.

Action: Blowback, selective fire

Caliber: 5.7×28mm

Type: Personal Defense Weapon (PDW)

Magazine Capacity: 50 rounds

Country of Origin: Belgium

Year Introduced: 1990

KRISS Vector

The KRISS Vector uses the unique Super V recoil system to reduce muzzle climb and recoil, making it highly controllable.

Action: Delayed blowback

Caliber: .45 ACP, 9×19mm, .40 S&W

Type: Tactical PDW / SMG

Magazine Capacity: 13, 17, or 30 rounds

Country of Origin: United States

Year Introduced: 2006

SIG MPX

The SIG MPX is a modern gas-operated submachine gun designed by SIG Sauer and widely used in tactical and law enforcement roles.

Action: Short-stroke gas piston

Caliber: 9×19mm Parabellum

Type: Modern SMG / PDW

Magazine Capacity: 10, 20, or 30 rounds

Country of Origin: Switzerland / USA

Year Introduced: 2011

Heckler & Koch UMP

The Heckler & Koch UMP is a lightweight and cost-effective successor to the MP5, chambered in several calibers.

Action: Blowback, selective fire

Caliber: .40 S&W, 9×19mm, .45 ACP

Type: Tactical SMG / PDW

Magazine Capacity: 25 or 30 rounds

Country of Origin: Germany

Year Introduced: 1999
