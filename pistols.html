Pistol Collection

Specifications, History, and Overview

Glock 17

The Glock 17 is a short recoil-operated, locked-breech semi-automatic pistol using a modified Browning cam-lock system adapted from the Hi-Power pistol.

Action: Short recoil, locked breech

Caliber: 9mm Parabellum

Type: Semi-automatic, striker-fired

Magazine Capacity: 17 rounds

Country of Origin: Austria

Year Introduced: 1982

Beretta 92

The Beretta 92 is a series of semi-automatic pistols designed and manufactured by Beretta of Italy. Production began in 1976 and it remains widely used today.

Action: Short recoil, locked breech

Caliber: 9mm Parabellum

Type: Semi-automatic, double/single action

Magazine Capacity: 15 rounds

Country of Origin: Italy

Year Introduced: 1976

Colt 1911

The Colt 1911 is a semi-automatic pistol chambered in .45 ACP and is one of the most iconic handgun designs in history.

Action: Short recoil, locked breech

Caliber: .45 ACP

Type: Semi-automatic, single action

Magazine Capacity: 7–8 rounds

Country of Origin: United States

Year Introduced: 1911

SIG Sauer P226

The SIG Sauer P226 is a full-sized service pistol known for reliability and use by military and law enforcement units worldwide.

Action: Short recoil, locked breech

Caliber: 9mm Parabellum

Type: Semi-automatic, double/single action

Magazine Capacity: 15–20 rounds

Country of Origin: Germany / USA

Year Introduced: 1984

Desert Eagle

The Desert Eagle is a gas-operated semi-automatic pistol famous for chambering powerful cartridges like the .50 Action Express.

Action: Gas-operated, rotating bolt

Caliber: .50 AE, .44 Magnum, .357 Magnum

Type: Semi-automatic

Magazine Capacity: 7–10 rounds

Country of Origin: Israel

Year Introduced: 1983
