Assault Rifles Collection

Specifications, History, and Overview

AK-47

The AK-47 is one of the most iconic assault rifles ever created, designed by Mikhail Kalashnikov in the Soviet Union and chambered for the 7.62×39mm cartridge.

Action: Gas-operated, rotating bolt

Caliber: 7.62×39mm

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 30-round detachable magazine

Country of Origin: Soviet Union

Year Introduced: 1947

M16

The M16 is a lightweight, gas-operated rifle developed for the United States military and chambered in 5.56×45mm NATO.

Action: Gas-operated, rotating bolt

Caliber: 5.56×45mm NATO

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 20/30-round detachable magazine

Country of Origin: United States

Year Introduced: 1964

M4 Carbine

The M4 Carbine is a compact version of the M16A2 rifle and is widely used by military forces around the world.

Action: Gas-operated, rotating bolt

Caliber: 5.56×45mm NATO

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 30-round detachable magazine

Country of Origin: United States

Year Introduced: 1990s

Heckler & Koch G36

The G36 is a German assault rifle developed in the 1990s and chambered in 5.56×45mm NATO.

Action: Short-stroke piston

Caliber: 5.56×45mm NATO

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 30-round detachable magazine

Country of Origin: Germany

Year Introduced: 1997

HK416

The HK416 is a highly reliable assault rifle developed by Heckler & Koch using a short-stroke gas piston system.

Action: Short-stroke piston

Caliber: 5.56×45mm NATO

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 20/30-round magazines

Country of Origin: Germany

Year Introduced: 2004

AK-74

The AK-74 is the successor to the AKM and uses the lighter 5.45×39mm cartridge for improved accuracy and control.

Action: Gas-operated, rotating bolt

Caliber: 5.45×39mm

Type: Assault Rifle

Magazine: 30-round detachable magazine

Country of Origin: Soviet Union

Year Introduced: 1974
